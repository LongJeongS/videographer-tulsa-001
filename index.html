From first call to final files in four steps

This is the part most buyers want to know before they call, so here it is in plain terms.

  1. Kickoff With Your Producer

    A call about what the video is for, who will see it, and where it will run. You get a written scope, timeline, and estimate before anything is booked.

  2. Script, Shot List, Schedule

    We write or refine the script, build the shot list, scout your location, and lock a shoot date that works around your operation.

  3. Shoot Day at Your Location

    The crew arrives with camera, lighting, and sound, and works around your staff and customers. Most business videos need one shoot day; larger pieces take two.

  4. Edit, Review, Deliver

    You review a cut, give notes, and we revise within the rounds set in the scope, usually one to two weeks after the shoot. Final files arrive in the formats agreed at kickoff.

Video Production in Tulsa: Questions Buyers Ask First

Straight answers on cost, timelines, what you receive, and where the crew travels, before you request an estimate.

How much does a commercial video cost in Tulsa?

Tulsa-area production companies publish a wide range: roughly $5,000 for short-form social content up to $40,000 and more for a full commercial with actors, multiple locations, and a broadcast finish. Most corporate, energy, and healthcare videos in the market land somewhere between $7,500 and $20,000. Those are typical market figures, not our prices. Your number depends on shoot days, script complexity, and how many versions you need, and it goes in a written estimate before anything is booked.

How long does it take to produce a brand video from kickoff to final delivery?

Most business videos in Tulsa take two to four weeks from kickoff to final files: about a week of pre-production for script, shot list, and scheduling, one or two shoot days, and one to two weeks of editing and revisions. A simple social piece can move faster. A commercial with casting and several locations runs longer. Your written scope carries the actual timeline for your project.

Do you travel to our location in Broken Arrow or Owasso, or do we need to come to you?

We come to you. Every shoot happens at your business, whether that is a warehouse in Broken Arrow, a clinic in Owasso, or a storefront in midtown Tulsa, because the point of most business video is showing the real place and the real people. The crew brings camera, lighting, and sound, and works around your hours. Anywhere within about 30 miles of downtown Tulsa is standard territory.

What do we get at the end, and will the video work on Instagram, YouTube, and TV?

You receive final files cut and formatted for the places you told us it will run. That usually means a widescreen master for your website and YouTube, vertical and square versions for Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn, and a broadcast-spec file if the spot is going to TV. Captions and shorter cutdowns are set in the scope at kickoff so nothing has to be re-edited later.

Do we need a script, or do you write it?

Either works. Many Tulsa clients arrive with a rough idea and a few bullet points, and the producer turns that into a script or interview outline you approve before the shoot. If your marketing team already has a script, we refine it for the camera and build the shot list from it. Script approval happens before a shoot date is locked.
